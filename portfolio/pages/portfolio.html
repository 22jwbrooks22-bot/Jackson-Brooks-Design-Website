Biography

I am a third-year architecture student at California Baptist University, developing a practice around craft, tradition, and the built vernacular. My work asks how space can carry meaning earned over centuries — how a building can embody intention without explaining itself.

I am drawn to architecture as civic and sacred art: buildings that emerge from their communities rather than being imposed on them. American historical styles, African and Middle Eastern vernacular, and a deep commitment to observational drawing shape both my academic work and independent study.

I  ·  Studio Project
Poetry
Foundation
Civic Cultural Center · Chicago, IL
CourseDesign Studio II · 2026
TypeCivic / Cultural
MediumAutoCAD, SketchUp, Graphite

A civic cultural institution designed to serve the Poetry Foundation's mission of making poetry accessible to the public. The project centers a public garden, reading room, and gallery within a single cohesive form, organized around procession and the threshold between city and interior. The design draws on the Chicago classical tradition while maintaining a restraint appropriate to its literary character.

II  ·  Studio Project
Schindler
Addition
Residential Addition · West Hollywood, CA
CourseDesign Studio I · 2025
TypeResidential / Addition
MediumAutoCAD, Physical Model, Graphite

A residential addition to the Schindler House in West Hollywood, one of American modernism's most studied domestic works. The project investigates how a new structure can extend an existing landmark without imitating it, introducing new program while responding to Schindler's material palette, courtyard logic, and relationship to the California landscape.
